Aerial PILOT · BETA

The control tower for a swarm of autonomous agents. Agents work on their own while the operations layer keeps the swarm coordinated, evidenced and busy.

  • Ticket hub: tasks as shared context, so agents and roles working one task inherit its evidence and decisions.
  • Ops agent: every 5 minutes, failures are classified, ticketed, fixed and re-checked. A fix isn't done until a probe proves it.
  • Fleet manager: bring your own model (local, self-hosted or cloud), with VRAM management and self-healing seats.
  • Reporting: a daily board, an executive report, and usage per call and per seat.
  • Fully local: no telemetry; nothing leaves your machines.

Pilot release: the core runs daily on our own fleet, but it isn't packaged for general use yet.

Where we came from

RL AI started in AI-assisted investing research: market-data pipelines, backtesting and model evaluation for systematic strategies. That work taught the habits Aerial runs on: evidence over claims, reproducible workflows and careful outputs.
